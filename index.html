Welcome to RB Khata

RB Khata is a digital accounting and ledger management application designed to help businesses manage customer accounts, transactions and records.

About RB Khata

RB Khata helps business owners maintain customer ledgers, track payments, manage transactions and organize their business records digitally.

Features

  • Customer account management
  • Digital ledger and transaction records
  • Business reports
  • PDF statements and receipts
  • Google Drive backup and restore

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

RB Khata respects the privacy of its users. We use information only to provide and improve the application's accounting and backup features.

Information and Data

Business records, customer details and transaction information entered into the application are used to provide its services.

Google Sign-In and Google Drive

Users can connect their Google Account to use Google Drive backup features. When a user requests a backup, the application uploads the backup file to the user's Google Drive.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect user information and business records.

Data Sharing

We do not sell users' personal information.

Contact

For questions about RB Khata and this Privacy Policy, please contact the application developer through the support email listed in the Google Play listing.
